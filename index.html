Welcome to Kirby Soft and Wet! In this game, you will help Kirby catch as many fish as he can.


All you have to do is throw the bobber at the right time and you can catch a variety of different fish!


Sometimes, you might not catch a fish at all! You might catch a squid, a shoe, or maybe even a reference from other Nintendo games!


Can you collect all the fish and help Kirby complete his catalog? Play Kirby Soft and Wet Now!


Kirby

First Appearance: Kirby's Dream Land (1992)

Kirby is the hero of this game as well as all other of his adventures in the past.
With the ability to float in the air and copy the powers of anyone he inhales, he is unstoppable!
He is one of the characters you can play as to catch both fish and references!


Waddle Dee

First Appearance: Kirby's Dream Land (1992)

Waddle Dees, depending on which one, can be either Kirby's greatest ally or his most common enemy.
Making his first appearance in Kirby's Dream Land, he is one of the most common characters in the Kirby franchise.
There is one special bandana-wearing, spear-wielding Waddle Dee that reformed in Kirby's Return to Dream Land, and has been one of Kirby's closest friends ever since.
Who knows? Maybe this particular Waddle Dee is that spear-wielding friend, he is just wielding a fishing pole this time!

Marx

First Appearance: Kirby Super Star (1996)

This seemingly friendly jester may appear an ally at first, but in reality, he seeks to rule Planet Popstar!
When he first met Kirby, he asked him to help stop the sun and moon from fighting, but there is much more to that crisis than he is letting on.
Tell you what, I'll just let you play Kirby Super Star and/or Super Star Ultra so you can see for yourself without spoilers. With an arsenal of magical powers up his sleeve, he is one of Kirby's toughest enemies.

Gooey

First Appearance: Kirby's Dream Land 2 (1995)

This adorable little blob may look strange at first, but don't let his appearance fool you—Gooey is actually one of Kirby's closest friends!
Gooey first appeared in Kirby's Dream Land 2, but he takes on a much more important role in Kirby's Dream Land 3, where he can accompany Kirby throughout his adventure.
Although he comes from the mysterious Dark Matter, Gooey is a kindhearted and playful companion who is always ready to help Kirby save Dream Land.
With his long, stretchy tongue and unique ability to swallow enemies and copy their powers, Gooey can hold his own in a fight. Whether he's helping Kirby on an adventure or just being his goofy self, Gooey is always happy to lend a hand!
